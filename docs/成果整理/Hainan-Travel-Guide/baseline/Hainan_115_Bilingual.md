### 001. 龙兴路 · Longxing Road

**位置**：海口市龙华区国贸片区的龙兴路

**中文**：海口市龙华区国贸片区的龙兴路。这条不到500米的小街最近成了很火的街拍地，很多博主和年轻帅哥美女专程来拍照。咖啡馆的门头、绿植、外摆桌椅和复古小店都很上镜，下午光线好，随便走走就容易出片。

**English**: Longxing Road is in the Guomao area of Longhua District, Haikou. This short street has become one of Haikou's favorite photo spots. Influencers and stylish young locals come for the pretty storefronts, outdoor cafés, and vintage shops. It's especially photogenic in the afternoon.

### 002. 滨海新村 · Binhai Xincun Neighborhood

**位置**：海口市龙华区滨海大道附近的滨海新村老社区

**中文**：海口市龙华区滨海大道附近的滨海新村老社区。这里的魅力不是大景区，而是老居民楼之间冒出来的小众咖啡馆和有趣店面。旧墙、窄巷和新潮门头放在一起，很适合拍一点自然的生活感照片。

**English**: You can find it at Binhai Xincun, an older neighborhood near Binhai Avenue in Haikou's Longhua District. The contrast is what makes it fun: old apartment blocks, narrow alleys, and trendy little cafés next door. It feels more like discovering a local neighborhood than visiting a tourist attraction.

### 003. 骑楼老街 · Qilou Old Street

**位置**：海口市龙华区中山路一带，靠近海口钟楼

**中文**：海口市龙华区中山路一带，靠近海口钟楼。最值得看的是两侧一整排带骑楼走廊的南洋老房子，和东南亚不少城市的老街有点像。白天适合拍建筑，边走边尝海南粉、清补凉和传统点心。

**English**: You can find it at Zhongshan Road in Longhua District, near Haikou Clock Tower. The long rows of arcaded buildings have a Southeast Asian feel you don't see everywhere in China. Look up at the old balconies, take street photos, and stop for Hainanese noodles or a sweet local dessert.

### 004. 云洞图书馆 · Cloud Cave Library

**位置**：海口市龙华区世纪广场路1号，海口湾海边

**中文**：海口市龙华区世纪广场路1号，海口湾海边。海边这栋弯弯曲曲的白色建筑就像雕塑，弧线门洞和大海放在同一张照片里特别漂亮。很多人傍晚来拍日落，想进馆阅读则要查看预约安排。

**English**: It is at No. 1 Century Square Road in Longhua District, right beside Haikou Bay. The white curved building looks almost like a sculpture beside the water. Its openings frame the sea beautifully, and sunset is a popular time for photos. Check the booking rules if you want to go inside.

### 005. 天空之山 · Sky Mountain

**位置**：海口市美兰区美丽沙路附近、海甸五西路海岸

**中文**：海口市美兰区美丽沙路附近、海甸五西路海岸。它不像普通的观海平台，更像一条能走上屋顶的白色海边艺术建筑。沿着弯曲步道慢慢走，能从不同角度拍海和建筑，顺便在附近喝咖啡。

**English**: It is near Meilisha Road and Haidian Fifth West Road in Haikou's Meilan District. This isn't a typical seaside lookout. You can follow the curved walkway over an unusual white building, finding different angles for the sea and architecture, then grab a coffee nearby.

### 007. 荣山寮村 · Rongshanliao Village

**位置**：海口市秀英区西秀镇荣山寮村，西海岸一带

**中文**：海口市秀英区西秀镇荣山寮村，西海岸一带。这里原本是渔村，现在除了渔船，还能看到沙滩鱼市和越来越多的海边休闲小店。傍晚一边看日落一边逛渔获市场，氛围比普通海滩更有生活味。

**English**: You can find it at Rongshanliao Village in Xixiu Town, Xiuying District, on Haikou's west coast. This is still a working fishing village, with boats and a beachside fish market alongside newer hangout spots. Come toward sunset to see the local catch and enjoy the warm evening light.

**位置**：海口市美兰区灵山镇美庄村，靠近江东新区白驹大道

### 009. 雷琼火山口地质公园 · Haikou Volcano Park

**位置**：海口市秀英区石山镇，靠近火山口公园景区入口

**中文**：海口市秀英区石山镇，靠近火山口公园景区入口。海口不只有海滩，城市西边还有古老的火山群。可以沿着山路走到火山口，看看黑色火山岩和长满植物的火山地形，和海南海边风景完全不同。

**English**: You can find it at Shishan Town in Xiuying District, at the Haikou volcano scenic area. Haikou isn't only about beaches—it also has ancient volcanic landscapes. Walk toward the crater, see the dark rock, and enjoy a very different side of the island.

### 010. 北港岛 · Beigang Island

**位置**：海口市美兰区演丰镇北港岛，东寨港海岸附近

**中文**：海口市美兰区演丰镇北港岛，东寨港海岸附近。岛上保留着渔船和渔村生活，退潮时大片滩涂会露出来。可以在安全区域观察小螃蟹、贝类和赶海的人，但一定要看潮汐，不要往深滩走。

**English**: You can find it at Beigang Island in Yanfeng Town, Meilan District, close to Dongzhai Harbor. This quiet fishing island is especially interesting at low tide, when wide mudflats appear. You might see small crabs, shells, and locals collecting seafood. Always check the tides and stay in safe areas.
